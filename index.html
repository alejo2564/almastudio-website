who we are

the studio.

alma studio is a two-person ios app studio built on a simple belief: software should feel as good as it works. we obsess over the details — the transitions, the typography, the moments between taps — because we think the best apps are the ones you barely notice you're using.

al

alejandro linares.

co-founder

by day, an investment banker in new york. by night, a builder. alejandro started alma studio out of a conviction that the tools and taste developed in finance could be channeled into making software that actually matters to people.

mc

maria cabrera.

co-founder

also a new york-based banker, maria co-founded alma studio to build products rooted in real interests rather than market trends. she brings sharp judgment and a belief that the best apps are born from genuine curiosity, not spreadsheets.
